# CarCheck — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 7, 2026**

Practical Apps ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CarCheck mobile application ("CarCheck" or the "app").

This Privacy Policy explains what information CarCheck handles and how it is used.

## Information CarCheck stores

CarCheck is designed to work locally on your device and does not require you to create an account.

Information you enter into an inspection may include:

* Vehicle details
* Checklist results and statuses
* Inspection notes
* Assessment and overall notes
* Photos taken or selected for an inspection
* Other information you choose to enter into the app

This information is stored locally on your device.

## How your information is used

The information you enter into CarCheck is used to help you document and organize your observations while inspecting a used vehicle.

CarCheck can use your inspection information to create a PDF inspection report containing information such as vehicle details, inspection results, notes, photos, and an observational-report disclaimer.

## Photos and camera

CarCheck may allow you to take or select photos for an inspection.

Photos are used as part of your inspection records and reports. They are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or otherwise export them through functionality provided by the app or your device.

CarCheck does not use your photos for advertising, profiling, or other unrelated purposes.

## Sharing reports

CarCheck allows you to share generated inspection reports.

Sharing is an action initiated by you. When you choose to share a report, the report is passed to the application or service you select through your device's sharing functionality.

Once you share a report with another application, person, or service, that recipient's own privacy policy and terms apply.

## Information we do not collect

CarCheck does not require an account and, as designed, does not collect your inspection information on a Practical Apps server.

We do not sell your inspection information or use it for advertising or profiling.

## Data retention and deletion

Inspection information is stored locally on your device.

You can manage or delete your inspection information using the functionality provided by CarCheck. You can also remove the app from your device, subject to how your device manages application data and backups.

Because CarCheck does not maintain a cloud account containing your inspection records, Practical Apps cannot retrieve or restore inspection information stored only on your device.

## Third-party services

CarCheck does not intentionally send your inspection information to third-party services operated by Practical Apps.

When you use your device's sharing functionality, information may be transferred to the application or service you select. Those services operate independently and are responsible for their own privacy practices.

## Children's privacy

CarCheck is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through CarCheck.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
